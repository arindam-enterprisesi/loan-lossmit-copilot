## 5303.1 | Overtime and bonus income

Overtime and bonus income may be used when there is a history of receipt of at least two years. A history of at least 12 months may be acceptable when the file documents positive factors that support continuance. The income is averaged over the history, and when it is declining the lender must determine whether it is stable before using it.

## 5302.2 | Pay stubs and employment verification

The most recent pay stub must be dated no earlier than 30 days before the application date and include year-to-date earnings. W-2s for the most recent two years, or a written verification of employment, support the employment history.

## 5501.3 | Asset verification and large deposits

Bank statements must cover the most recent two months and include all pages. A deposit that is large relative to the borrower's income, or not consistent with the account's history, must have its source documented. Funds that cannot be sourced are not used to qualify.

## 5401.2 | Monthly debt payment-to-income ratio

The debt payment-to-income ratio compares the borrower's monthly debt payments, including the housing payment, with stable monthly income. Every revolving account's minimum payment is counted, and installment debts with more than ten monthly payments remaining are counted.

## 5203.1 | Credit score indicator

The indicator score is the middle of three scores, the lower of two, or the single score reported.

## LO-1 | Variable income history

The sample lender counts overtime and bonus income only when 24 months of history are documented. Twelve to 24 months of history is not used under this overlay, even where an agency guide allows it.

## LO-2 | Debt-to-income review level

A debt-to-income ratio above 43 percent is referred for a second review, with the compensating factors written in the file.

## LO-3 | Large deposits

Any single non-payroll deposit of 1,000.00 or more must have its source documented, whatever the borrower's income.

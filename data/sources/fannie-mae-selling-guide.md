## B3-3.1-01 | General income information: stable and continuing income

The lender must determine that the borrower's income is stable, predictable and likely to continue. In general a two-year history of receipt is expected for each source of income. The lender documents the history and judges whether the income is likely to continue for at least three years.

## B3-3.1-01.variable | Variable income: overtime and bonus

Overtime and bonus income may be used when the borrower has received it for at least two years. A history of 12 to 24 months may be acceptable when the lender can justify and document why the income is likely to continue. The lender averages the income over the period received, and when the income is declining the lender must analyse the reason and use the lower, current figure or exclude it.

## B3-3.1-02 | Employment documentation: pay stubs and W-2s

A recent pay stub must be dated no earlier than 30 days before the initial loan application and must show year-to-date earnings. The lender also obtains W-2 forms covering the most recent two years, or an acceptable alternative such as a written verification of employment.

## B3-4.2-01 | Verification of deposit accounts

Deposit accounts are verified with bank statements covering the most recent two full months. Every page of each statement must be provided, including pages that show no transactions. The statements must identify the account holder, the account and the ending balance.

## B3-4.2-02 | Large deposits

For a purchase, the lender must document the source of any large deposit, defined as a single deposit that exceeds 50 percent of the total monthly qualifying income. Payroll deposits and transfers between the borrower's own verified accounts do not need further documentation. An unsourced large deposit is subtracted from the assets used to qualify.

## B3-4.3-04 | Gifts

Gift funds from an acceptable donor must be documented with a gift letter signed by the donor that states the amount, the donor's relationship to the borrower, and that no repayment is expected. The lender also verifies the transfer of the funds from the donor to the borrower.

## B3-5.1-02 | Representative credit score

When three credit scores are reported for a borrower, the representative score is the middle score; when two are reported, it is the lower score; when one is reported, it is that score.

## B3-6-02 | Debt-to-income ratios

The debt-to-income ratio is the borrower's total monthly debt obligations, including the proposed housing payment, divided by the total monthly qualifying income. Maximum ratios depend on the loan program and the underwriting method; ratios above a lender's review level call for compensating factors documented in the file.

## B3-6-05 | Monthly debt obligations

Monthly debt obligations include the proposed housing expense and the minimum required payment on each revolving account, even when the account will be paid off. Installment debts with more than ten months of payments remaining are included. Accounts that appear on the credit report but not in the application must be considered.
